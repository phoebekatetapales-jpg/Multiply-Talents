Core BIM capabilities

From design intent to construction reality.

Our BIM and VDC services align with the natural flow of a project, ensuring continuity from design intent to construction reality.

01

3D Modeling (Architecture, Structure, MEP)

Creating parametric models that serve as the single source of truth for the project lifecycle.

02

Clash Detection & Coordination

Using Navisworks to detect and report clashes between trades early, ensuring construction issues are resolved before building begins.

03

Scan-to-BIM (Point Cloud Modeling)

Converting laser scan data into accurate Revit models for renovation and retrofit projects (As-Built creation).

04

Revit Family Creation

Building custom, parametric component libraries for manufacturers and design firms.
